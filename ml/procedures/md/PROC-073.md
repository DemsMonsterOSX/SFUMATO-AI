# PROC-073 Insider data theft

## Detection signals
- Bulk downloads before a resignation
- Copying to USB or personal accounts

## Triage
- Involve HR and legal before any action
- Preserve logs and device images
- Scope what data was accessed

## Containment
- Restrict access in coordination with HR

## Eradication
- Recover data and remove copies where lawful

## Recovery
- Review leaver and offboarding processes

## Evidence to keep
- Access and transfer logs, device audit

## Possible false positives
- Normal work during a project handover
