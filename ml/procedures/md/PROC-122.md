# PROC-122 Insufficient evidence to decide

## Detection signals
- Sparse alert with few fields or no enrichment possible

## Triage
- List the missing facts and who can provide them
- Do not escalate or contain on speculation
- Set a review time

## Containment
- Monitor only

## Eradication
- Not applicable

## Recovery
- Reopen when new data arrives

## Evidence to keep
- Alert and the list of missing information

## Possible false positives
- Not applicable
