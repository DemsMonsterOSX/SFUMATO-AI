# PROC-125 Personal data breach notification

## Detection signals
- Personal data of individuals was accessed or exposed

## Triage
- Involve the data protection officer immediately
- Document nature, categories and volume of data and the likely consequences
- Check the applicable notification deadline with legal

## Containment
- Stop the exposure

## Eradication
- Remove the cause

## Recovery
- Notify authorities and individuals as legally required, track remediation

## Evidence to keep
- Breach register entry

## Possible false positives
- Not applicable
